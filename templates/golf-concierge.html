FAQs

Everything you need to know about Caddie

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out to our team any time.

What is Caddie?

Caddie is an AI golf concierge. It watches tee sheets across every course near you and books the round you want, at the time and price you set, without you refreshing anything.

Which courses does it cover?

Public, resort, and many private guest tee sheets. Connect the courses you play and Caddie watches them all from one place.

How does it book for me?

Set your preferences once. When an opening matches, Caddie holds and confirms it, checks your group's calendars, and drops the round onto yours.

What does it cost?

Caddie is free while in open beta. Green fees are always paid to the course directly, Caddie never marks them up.

Can I set a budget?

Yes. Give Caddie a price ceiling and it only books rounds that fit, or waits for a twilight rate.
