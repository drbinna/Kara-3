Frequently asked questions

Do you sell signals?
No, and I never will. Signals make you dependent; process makes you independent. Everything I teach is aimed at you not needing me.
What markets do you trade?
FX majors, gold, and index futures — with occasional crypto when structure is clean. The playbook matters more than the market.
Who is the mentorship for?
Traders who've been burned by hype and want a repeatable, risk-first process. Complete beginners are welcome if they're patient; get-rich-quick hunters are not.
How do I join the next cohort?
Email hello@rosyargent.com with a short note on where you are in your trading journey. Cohorts are kept small on purpose.
