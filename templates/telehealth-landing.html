?Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, answered

Is Subcare health insurance?
No. Subcare is a healthcare membership that covers your everyday care: provider visits, care plans, and med delivery. It pairs well with a high-deductible plan for emergencies.
Are the providers real licensed doctors?
Yes. Every Subcare provider is licensed in your state, and you keep the same provider so they actually know you.
What if I need in-person care?
Your plan includes an annual in-person checkup, and Premium members get same-day appointments at partner clinics near them.
Can I really cancel anytime?
Anytime, in two clicks, no phone call required. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you paid for.
