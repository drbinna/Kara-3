Frequently asked questions

If you have anything else you want to ask, reach out to us.

Does Pace work with my watch?

Yes. Pace pairs with Apple Watch, Garmin, Coros, and any Bluetooth heart-rate strap.

Do I need to run fast to use it?

Not at all. Pace is built for recreational runners first. Your easy pace is the whole point.

How do crew challenges work?

Invite friends, pick a weekly goal, and the leaderboard updates live after every run.

Does it work offline?

Runs record fully offline and sync when you're back on a network. Music keeps playing.

Can I import my old runs?

Yes, bring your full history from Strava, Nike Run Club, or a GPX export in one tap.

What about battery life?

A 60-minute run uses about 4 percent battery with GPS and the pace coach on.
