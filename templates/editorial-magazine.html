What we lost when everything got fast

An essay on speed, attention, and the case for taking the scenic route.

Somewhere between the overnight delivery and the instant answer, we stopped noticing the seams. Speed became the default setting of everything, and the things that refused to hurry started to look like defects rather than choices. This essay is about those things, and the people who still make them.
